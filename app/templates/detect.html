System Architecture & Pipeline

📥

1. DICOM / CT Normalization

Standardizes contrast, applies Hounsfield windowing, and resizes slices to backbone tensor input dimensions.

🧠

2. Transfer Learning

Deep residual feature hierarchies trained on abdominal scans extract subtle pancreatic tissue irregularities.

📊

3. Explainable Diagnosis

Computes class-activation gradients (Grad-CAM) to localize the morphological features driving classification.
